Factory Upgrades & Enhancements

OEM Retrofit & Integration

Elevate your vehicle with genuine manufacturer hardware options: digital cockpits, adaptive LED lighting, reversing cameras, and modern infotainment coded seamlessly to factory specifications.

Factory Technology, Flawlessly Integrated

When purchasing a new or used vehicle, missing out on desired optional extras—such as an Audi Virtual Cockpit, BMW M-Sport steering wheel with paddle shifters, or a high-definition reversing camera—often feels irreversible. Main dealers will almost never perform retrofits due to strict corporate procedures.

At Car ECU Solutions, our automotive electricians specialize in OEM retrofits. We source genuine factory parts, install dedicated vehicle harnesses into original fuse carriers, and reconfigure central gateway modules so the vehicle operates exactly as if built on the factory assembly line with that equipment.

Popular Retrofit Upgrades We Deliver

Digital Instrument Clusters / Virtual Cockpits (Audi, VW, BMW 6WB)
OEM High-Definition Reversing Cameras with dynamic steering trajectory lines
LED & Matrix Headlights with dynamic sweeping rear indicators
Apple CarPlay & Android Auto activation / MIB2 & NBT EVO navigation
Cruise Control & Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) radar module retrofits
Power folding electric mirrors & steering wheel heating / paddle shifters

The OEM Standard: No Wire Cutting or Scotch-Locks

Inferior aftermarket installations splice into existing wires with plastic scotch-locks, inducing voltage drops and dangerous parasitic battery drains.

We adhere strictly to OEM electrical standards: every wire is pinned into factory fuse boxes using genuine TE Connectivity automotive terminals, wrapped in heat-resistant fabric fleece tape, and integrated directly into the CAN-bus network.

Vehicle Order (VO) Update & Component Protection

Installing the hardware is only half the process. We modify the vehicle build order (VO in BMW, PR-codes in VAG) and execute online authorization to clear component protection. Even during future dealer services, your retrofitted features will remain fully recognized.
