Our Technical Method

More than a repair.
A clearer way forward.

Modern vehicles are distributed computing networks on wheels. When warning lights appear, dealerships often quote thousands to replace complete assemblies. We interrogate the exact hardware component or software register that failed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you visit.

Answers to common questions regarding our vehicle diagnostic and electronics service.

How does your diagnostic assessment differ from a generic OBD scan?
A generic code reader only displays generic OBD-II fault codes without manufacturer-specific sub-registers, live sensor graphs, or wiring context. At Car ECU Solutions, we connect dealer-level diagnostic protocols, analyze oscilloscope signal waveforms on CAN and LIN buses, test physical pin voltages, and diagnose the root cause rather than merely erasing error codes.
Can you repair my existing ECU or do I have to buy a brand new one?
Main dealerships almost exclusively quote for full replacement control units costing between £1,000 and £3,000+ plus coding. In over 80% of cases, we can repair the physical circuit board, replace blown mosfets or injector drivers, or clone the original EEPROM and microprocessor data onto a refurbished donor unit at a fraction of the cost.
Do you offer postal or mail-in repair services for ECU and airbag modules?
Yes. Mechanics, body shops, and vehicle owners across the UK send us ECUs, airbag SRS modules for crash data reset, and instrument clusters via Royal Mail Special Delivery. We inspect, repair, and test modules on our bench before returning them via tracked courier, often within 24 to 48 hours.
Will an ECU remap affect my vehicle's reliability or MOT compliance?
Our calibrations are precision-mapped within the safe thermal and mechanical tolerances established by the vehicle manufacturer. We do not apply aggressive generic files that overheat turbos or overload transmissions. All calibrated road maps comply with UK emissions and MOT testing standards.
What proof of ownership is required for immobiliser and key programming?
For legitimate vehicle security, spare keys, all-keys-lost programming, and immobiliser resets, we require government photo identification (driving licence or passport) along with the vehicle V5C logbook matching the registered owner before programming vehicle transponders.
