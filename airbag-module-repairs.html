Airbag Module Repairs & Reset

Resetting indelible crash data, internal controller faults, and deployment event records stored inside your SRS airbag ECU following an accident.

Why Airbag Modules Lock After Impact

During a collision, impact sensors trigger pyrotechnic pre-tensioners, steering airbags, curtain airbags, or pedestrian bonnet charges. In doing so, the SRS computer immediately writes permanent non-volatile fault records to its internal EEPROM or microcontroller.

Standard OBD diagnostic scanners cannot clear these hard codes. Dealerships routinely insist on purchasing a brand-new airbag computer (often exceeding £600 to £1,200) followed by extensive SCN programming.

How We Clear Crash Data & Restore Your Unit

At Car ECU Solutions, we connect directly to the circuit board via bench pinouts or surface contact points to read the raw memory bin file. We selectively scrub the collision event flags and deployment triggers while strictly retaining your vehicle’s factory coding, VIN number, and sensor calibration curves.

Once reinstalled into your vehicle alongside replaced airbags and seatbelts, the module powers on in its factory un-deployed state, clearing the red SRS warning lamp and ensuring complete MOT compliance.
