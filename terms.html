Service Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Guidelines governing appointment requests, initial diagnostic assessments, written quotations, workshop access, and automotive security programming.

1. Appointment Requests & Booking

Submitting a preferred date and time through our website represents an initial appointment enquiry. An appointment is only confirmed once our workshop engineer has assessed vehicle symptoms, confirmed technical tooling availability, and contacted you via telephone or email.

2. Diagnostic Assessment & Initial Fee

Initial diagnostic investigation entails technical technician time, oscilloscope waveform analysis, dealer software interrogation, and circuit testing. Our standard diagnostic assessment fee covers the time required to isolate the root cause and provide an informed quotation. No replacement components or repair procedures will be initiated without prior customer agreement.

3. Proof of Vehicle Ownership

In accordance with UK motor trade anti-theft standards, all requests for immobiliser resets, duplicate smart keys, transponder coding, and all-keys-lost emergency work require the registered keeper to present a valid V5C vehicle logbook and matching photographic government identification (driving licence or passport) prior to programming.

4. Quotations & Repair Authorization

All repair quotations issued following diagnostic testing remain valid for 14 calendar days. If secondary pre-existing faults (such as severed wiring looms, prior unauthorized solder attempts, or flood damage) are uncovered during disassembly, the customer will be provided with an updated written assessment before proceeding.

5. Postal / Mail-In Modules

Customers sending standalone engine ECUs, airbag SRS units, or keys to our Romford workshop for bench repair are responsible for secure packaging and insured transit. Return shipping is provided via Royal Mail Special Delivery or tracked courier upon completion and payment.
