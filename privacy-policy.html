Privacy Policy

This privacy policy outlines how Car ECU Solutions collects, handles, and safeguards personal information and vehicle diagnostic records.

1. Information We Collect

When you request an appointment or submit a diagnostic enquiry through this website, we collect personal contact details (your name, telephone number, and email address) alongside vehicle specifications (make, model, year of manufacture, vehicle registration mark, and fault descriptions).

2. Purpose of Processing

Your information is used strictly to evaluate diagnostic feasibility, schedule workshop access at our Romford premises, confirm repair quotations, and communicate with you regarding your vehicle’s assessment and collection.

3. No Payment Card Collection

This website does not process financial transactions or collect credit or debit card data. All payments for completed diagnostic investigations and module repairs are handled in person at our workshop or via formal business bank transfer following work authorization.

4. Security & Ownership Verification

For security-sensitive operations—such as immobiliser resetting, duplicate smart key cutting, and all-keys-lost programming—we visually inspect your V5C registration certificate and government identification solely to verify legitimate vehicle ownership. These security records are held in strict confidence in accordance with UK automotive trade standards.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain appointment communications only for the period necessary to service customer warranties and accounting obligations. Under UK GDPR legislation, you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your personal data.
