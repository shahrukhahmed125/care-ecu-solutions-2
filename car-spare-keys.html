Our Technical Programming Method

Programming a modern automotive key involves multi-tier electronic handshakes:

1. Mechanical Laser Cutting: Using computerized decoders, we cut high-security internal-track laser blades to exact factory depths, ensuring smooth operation in door locks and ignition barrels.
2. Security Data Extraction: On challenging vehicles (such as BMW CAS4/FEM, VAG MQB, or Mercedes FBS3/FBS4), we interface with the BCM or instrument cluster microprocessor on our test bench to calculate the component security (CS) bytes and PIN.
3. Pre-coding & Learning: We pre-code an OEM-quality transponder chip with your vehicle's cryptographic seed, introduce it via OBD diagnostics, and verify immediate start and central locking operation.

Vehicle Owner Verification

To prevent unauthorized vehicle duplication, all key cutting and transponder programming requires physical inspection of the registered owner’s V5C registration document and government photo ID.
